6. Check Active Towers KPI card — must equal initial count + 1 (e.g. 3).

**Expected Result:**
After activation + save: Active Towers KPI = initial + 1.
After cleanup + save: Active Towers KPI = initial (restored).
Count change reflects immediately on page navigation — no manual refresh needed.
