# CP Portal — Overview

The Channel Partner Portal is used by external referral agents (Channel Partners) registered with Growth Housing. CPs use this portal to manage their leads, register new customers on their behalf, assist with KYC document uploads, submit Joint Booking Proposals (JBPs), and share project information with prospects via QR codes and referral links.
