# Feature-Spec: Work Progress

**Portal:** Buyer Portal
**URL:** `https://uat.xrportal.in/work-progress`
**Created:** 2026-05-12
**Status:** Complete

---

## Feature 1: View Construction Progress

### 1.1 Objective

Allow buyers to view the current status of construction at the project site, including milestone photos and progress updates.

### 1.2 Scope

Read-only section. Content is managed by the admin via CMS. Available to all registered buyers.

### 1.3 Preconditions

- Buyer must be logged in

### 1.4 Content Displayed

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Construction milestone photos | Site photos showing progress at each stage |
| Progress updates | Stage-wise descriptions of what has been completed |
| Milestone dates | When each construction stage was reached |

### 1.5 Business Rules

1. Content is managed via CMS by the admin team
2. Read-only — buyers cannot add comments or content
3. Available to buyers at all stages of the journey (pre- and post-allocation)

---

## How to Use: Viewing Construction Progress

**Who does this:** Buyer, to stay informed about project construction

---

**Step 1 — Navigate to Work Progress**

From the navigation menu, click **Work Progress**. The construction update screen will load.

**Step 2 — Browse milestone photos and updates**

Scroll through the photos and descriptions showing what has been completed at the project site. Each update is labelled with the construction stage and date.

> **Updates are published by the developer's team.** If you want to know about a specific construction stage or have concerns, contact your Sales Manager or raise a Support Ticket.
