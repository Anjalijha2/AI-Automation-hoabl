# Buyer Portal — Overview

The Buyer Portal is the customer-facing web application used by registered buyers to participate in property allocation events, track their booking, complete KYC, manage milestone payments, apply for home loans, raise support tickets, and request sales manager callbacks. This is the primary touchpoint between Growth Housing and its customers.
