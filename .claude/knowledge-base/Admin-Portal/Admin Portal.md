# Admin Portal — Overview

The Admin Portal is the primary back-office control center for XR Portal. It is used by Growth Housing internal staff to manage the entire property sales lifecycle: from configuring towers and units, running allocation campaigns, managing customer registrations and KYC, to monitoring payments and configuring system-wide settings.
